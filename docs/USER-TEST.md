# ExitDrill first-use check

Status: prepared; no human sessions completed or results claimed.

Recruit five people who currently hold, or have held, ERC-4626 vault shares. Include at least two who do not write code. Do not ask for private keys or real transactions.

Give each person these tasks without explaining the interface:

1. Open the live demo and rehearse 25% of the test position.
2. Explain what PASS means and whether it guarantees a later withdrawal.
3. Export the kit, close the website, extract the files, and open the saved evidence.
4. Explain which parts need internet, Node.js, an RPC, or a wallet.
5. With a local test chain supplied, launch the kit and run a fresh preflight.

Record completion, time, help needed, exact points of confusion, and the participant's explanation. Ask when they last needed an alternative vault interface, what they did, and whether they would keep this kit. Do not ask only whether they like the idea.

Release target: all five distinguish historical evidence from a guarantee; at least four open offline evidence unaided; at least three complete a fresh preflight unaided. These are acceptance targets, not observed results.
